A video is analyzed once against the ITU-R BT.1702 broadcast flashing rules. Each hazard gets the gentlest gray veil that brings it within limits, the result is re-verified, and the plan ships as a small timed file next to the video. Any player can read it. This page is one of those players.

What happens when you press a profile

1. Download and hash

The page downloads the video and computes its SHA-256 in your browser.

2. Bind and verify

The HazardTrack must name that exact checksum and say its verifier passed. Otherwise playback is refused.

3. Schedule the veil

The 60-line reader drives a gray overlay from the video clock, with linear fades in and out.
